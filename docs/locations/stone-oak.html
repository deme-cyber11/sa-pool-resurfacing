Building a New Pool Inside San Antonio City Limits

For homes inside San Antonio city limits, the city's Development Services Department takes residential pool and spa permits through the BuildSA Customer Portal. Its published rules include:

  • A fence or barrier at least 48 inches high around any private pool, hot tub or spa more than 24 inches deep, with self-closing and self-latching gates and doors. A pool with a powered safety cover that meets ASTM F1346 does not need the barrier.
  • Setbacks of 5 feet from the side and rear property lines, measured from the inside wall of the pool at the waterline. Pools are not permitted in front of the property.
  • Separate permits for mechanical, electrical and plumbing work.
  • The barrier installed and inspected before the pool is filled and used.

The city's Information Bulletin 170, revised May 2025, says residential pools must meet the 2024 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code.
