02 — Discovery Questions: Site & Lead Tailoring

Questions to Ask Before Expanding the Site

These questions will let us prioritize which new pages to build and how to position the copy so leads match exactly what KZ wants to close.

  • 1
    Which renovation job do you close at the highest rate — resurfacing, tile & coping, or full redesigns? Tells us where to put the most content depth and conversion optimization.
  • 2
    What's your minimum project size for renovation leads? Do you have a hard floor? We can add qualification language to the form so sub-threshold leads self-filter out before reaching your inbox.
  • 3
    Do you want weekly cleaning leads, or only bigger renovation and construction work? Determines whether it's worth building a maintenance landing page for your territory.
  • 4
    Which zip codes or neighborhoods are your highest-close, highest-ticket areas in SA? We can build hyper-local pages (Stone Oak, Alamo Heights, Helotes, etc.) to dominate those pockets in map pack and organic.
  • 5
    Do you take commercial pool work — HOAs, hotels, apartment complexes? If yes, it justifies a commercial landing page. If no, we should add disqualifying language so we don't waste anyone's time on those inquiries.
  • 6
    What's the one type of call your team groans at receiving — the lead you never want? We'll add language on the site that actively disqualifies that caller before they submit a form.
  • 7
    Do you offer financing? Do customers ask about it before calling, or after the quote? If customers ask upfront, we need to mention it on the site. If only post-quote, it's a closer tool and we can skip it on the landing page.
  • 8
    What's your typical close rate on resurfacing inquiries vs. new construction? Helps us calibrate how aggressively to push volume vs. quality. High close rate = flood the zone. Low close rate = add more pre-qualification.
  • 9
    Are there specific finishes (Pebble Tec, quartz, tile brands) you want to feature or steer people toward? We can write content around those finishes to pre-sell customers on the premium options before they even call, which shortens your sales conversation.
  • 10
    How does a typical customer find you today — Google Search, Google Maps, referral, or something else? If most of their customers come from referral, we want to position Alamo as the digital capture for people who haven't heard of KZ yet — net new audience, not overlap.
03 — Business Location Expansion (GBP)

Google Business Profile — Secondary Address Question

KZ already operates out of four verified locations (San Antonio, New Braunfels, Kerrville, Corpus Christi). Each of those is a potential territory where we could build a separate rank-and-rent site or optimize existing pages to feed that specific office.

Ask KZ directly:

  • A
    Do you operate out of any secondary locations, storage yards, service facilities, or satellite offices — particularly on the south side of SA, Boerne side, or Hill Country? Even a consistent address where trucks park overnight or equipment is stored can qualify for a Google Business Profile. A second GBP in a different part of the metro significantly expands map pack coverage, especially in lower-competition suburbs.
  • B
    Does any team member (operations manager, field supervisor) work out of a home office or base that's in a different quadrant of the city than the main San Pedro Ave. location? A service-area business can use a practitioner's address. Not a workaround — Google explicitly allows this for service businesses with multiple coverage zones.
  • C
    Are there any pool supply partners, distributor relationships, or co-located businesses where KZ crew regularly stages work? Partner locations sometimes qualify if there's a genuine operational presence. Worth exploring if they have relationships with tile suppliers or plaster vendors with physical addresses.

Why this matters: KZ's main GBP at San Pedro Ave. wins map pack results for central/north SA searches. A second verified location south of Loop 410 or in New Braunfels captures entirely different search clusters without competing with their existing profile.

05 — Lead Reactivation System for KZ

The Opportunity: 40 Years of Sleeping Revenue

Keith Zars has been building pools since 1985. Their CRM contains thousands of past customers — pool builds from the 80s, 90s, and 2000s, renovation jobs, repair tickets, lapsed maintenance contracts, and lost quotes that never closed. Most of this contact history is sitting dormant, never touched after the job ended.

The math on KZ's dormant base: A pool built in 2010 is now 16 years old. White plaster lifespan is 8–12 years. A quartz finish runs 15–20. Conservatively, thousands of their past builds are statistically overdue for resurfacing, tile cleaning, or a full renovation upgrade right now. They just aren't being asked.

5 Reactivation Tracks We Can Build for KZ

🗓 Pool Age Milestone Triggers
Automated email sent at 8, 10, and 12 years after build or last resurface. Subject: "Your pool turns 10 this year — here's what to expect." Plants the resurfacing seed before the customer even notices a problem. Low-cost, high-ROI drip that runs without staff attention.
🧹 Tile Cleaning / Maintenance Upsell
Every pool built by KZ has tile. Calcium scale buildup starts showing at 3–5 years, especially with SA's hard water. A "your tile is due for a cleaning" email sent to anyone with a 5+ year-old build is a low-ticket, high-conversion reactivation. Service appointment → estimate conversation → renovation upsell.
🔧 Repair-to-Renovation Bridge
When a service tech closes a repair ticket, tag the customer for a 30-day follow-up: "Your equipment needed repair, which often means the surface is also showing wear — want a free inspection?" Converts service customers into renovation leads automatically. Requires a simple CRM tag + email trigger.
📅 Pre-Summer Inspection Campaign
February–March: "Before the SA heat hits, get your pool inspected — we'll check surface, tile, and equipment for free." The free inspection gets a KZ tech on-site at every customer's house. In-person = close rate multiplier. This campaign alone could generate 30–50 warm leads per season.
💬 Lost Quote Reactivation
Leads who got a quote but didn't sign in a prior year. "Prices have gone up industry-wide — your 2022 quote is still valid if you want to lock it in." Urgency + familiarity combo. These are the easiest closes because they already said yes once, just not right then.
⭐ Upgrade & Modernization Track
Target builds from 2005–2015 with a "pool modernization" angle: smart controls, LED lighting, sun shelf additions, pebble upgrades. Different frame than "resurfacing" — sounds aspirational, not maintenance. Targets homeowners who might not think their pool "needs" work but respond to an upgrade pitch. Higher ticket, longer close cycle.

What ITD Builds to Power This System

1. Lead reactivation landing page — A dedicated page on alamopoolresurfacing.com (or a standalone domain) targeted at SA homeowners whose pools are 8–15 years old. Captures organic search traffic for "pool resurfacing cost San Antonio" from homeowners who are already in the problem-aware stage. Feeds KZ directly.

2. Tile cleaning landing page — Targets "pool tile cleaning San Antonio" searches. Lower competition, easier to rank. Homeowner books a cleaning → KZ tech is on-site → renovation conversation happens naturally.

3. Email sequence system (CRM-agnostic) — If KZ shares a customer export (name, email, build year), we build an automated Brevo sequence that fires the milestone emails, inspection invites, and upgrade offers on schedule. Runs forever with zero ongoing effort from KZ staff.

4. "Your pool age" quiz/calculator — An interactive widget on the site: "Enter your pool's age and finish type — we'll tell you if it's time to resurface." Captures email + self-qualifies the lead in one step. Feeds both the organic site and the reactivation email list.

Questions to Unlock the Reactivation Conversation

  • 1
    Do you have a CRM with past customer build/install dates, or is that data in your service software? Tells us whether this is a simple export or requires custom data pulling. Most pool CRMs (Skimmer, ServiceTitan, even QuickBooks) can export a customer list with job type and date.
  • 2
    How often does your team currently reach back out to past customers proactively? Most pool companies: never. If the answer is "we send a newsletter sometimes," there's a massive gap we can fill with automated, triggered outreach instead of batch-and-blast.
  • 3
    What percentage of your annual renovation revenue comes from past KZ customers vs. new-to-KZ homeowners? Industry benchmark is 30–40% of service revenue from repeat/past customers. If they're under that, the reactivation opportunity is massive and easy to quantify in dollars.
  • 4
    Does your team do proactive inspection calls or do customers only reach out when something breaks? Reactive-only = money left on the table. If we build the inspection campaign funnel, we convert them from reactive to proactive — and ITD gets credit for every job that closes from it.
  • 5
    Would you be open to a pilot — a 90-day reactivation campaign to 200 past customers — to prove the ROI before committing to a full system? Low-risk proof of concept. We take a segment of their oldest builds (2008–2013), run a 3-email sequence, and track inspection appointments booked and jobs closed. One closed renovation at $6,000+ pays for the pilot and then some.

Revenue Projection: What the Reactivation System Is Worth

Conservative scenario — targeting only past builds, no lapsed quotes:

Assumptions: KZ has built ~3,000 pools since 1985. Roughly 800 of those are statistically overdue for resurfacing (builds from 2005–2015). Average resurfacing job: $6,500. Industry email reactivation close rate for in-network past customers: 4–8%.

Low end: 800 contacts × 4% = 32 jobs × $6,500 avg = $208,000 in renovation revenue from one campaign to a list they already own.

High end: 800 contacts × 8% = 64 jobs × $8,000 avg (many will upgrade to quartz/pebble) = $512,000 in revenue.

ITD's angle: we build the system, we track attribution, we charge per closed job that comes through our funnel — same model as the current lead agreement.
